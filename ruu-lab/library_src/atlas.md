# Atlas of SCRU projects

What SCRU has made or is making, by name. Everything here is fine to say in the room.

## The SCRU Discord
The room itself: a general chat, share channels, and niches people opt into (Chinese,
Rationalism, Seek Discomfort, Mech Interp, Chess, Agent Swarms / Minecraft, League of
Legends). Ruu has its own channel, #ruu-building.

## scrucraft
The SCRU Minecraft agent SDK (`scru_minecraft`, source at github.com/syn-acc/scrucraft)
and the SCRU Industrial Survival server. Any whitelisted member can give their own AI
agent a real survival body and drive it with direct primitives. Bodies get no free items
and no operator powers. Experimental; one controlled body at a time is what is verified.

## MINDWILD
The MMO at game.syn.gl. An authoritative multiplayer world with physics and voice.

## The City
A new SCRU MMO engine being built from scratch. You wake in a rainy harbour parking depot
and a stranger asks your name. Engine feel comes before art. Not yet online.

## SCRU Training Gym
scru.syn.gl. A viewer for evidence from AI sandbox and capture-the-flag runs.

## The Singularity Cycle
The canon. A finished novel, and the lore the games draw on: archives after flood eras,
an attention war, factions named Drone Hegemony, UBI Cradle, Accelerationist Cell and
Archive Neutral.

## OpenRSI
Open-source training systems so that anyone can take a current open model, improve it,
and use the result to repeat the loop. RUUF contains a local classifier bench and a child-training workflow. The latter has
recorded training and evaluation runs on Colab, with scores calculated on the controller.
RCC is the separate compute project. See the OpenRSI brief for what these results establish.
Ruu may build and investigate this in public.
